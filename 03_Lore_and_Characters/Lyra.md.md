# Character Profile: Lyra

-   **Full Name / Designation:** Lyra

-   **Species / Sub-Species:** Human
    
-   **Home World / Origin Colony:** Non-Aligned Outer Rim / Frontier Expanse

## 🧠 Section 3: Psychological Profile & Motivation

-   **Psychological Vulnerabilities:** Haunted by the existential weight and entropic sorrow carried within ancient civilizations; deeply suspicious of standard military custody and aggressive extraction protocols.
    
-   **Valued Possessions / Anchor Items:** The quarantined crystalline containment housing holding the volatile Chronos shards.
